What are some high-protein vegetarian breakfasts?

✳
NUTRIRAG · A MOMENT AGO

Absolutely! A few satisfying options to try:

  • Greek yogurt bowl with berries, nuts, and a sprinkle of seeds.
  • Eggs with whole-grain toast and sautéed spinach or tomatoes.
  • Tofu scramble with black beans, peppers, and avocado.

Protein amounts vary by ingredient and portion. Mix it up with foods you enjoy—there’s no single “perfect” breakfast.
